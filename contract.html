The scenario

Procurement needs the whole picture before accepting a new deal

A supplier submits a proposed supply agreement, order form, and updated product schedule. The agreement is deposited in a monitored OpenText Core Content Management workspace; that event starts an assessment orchestrated from OpenText Aviator Studio.

BUYER CONTEXT

Innovate Wholesale

Innovate buys computer equipment and supplies for resale. Procurement wants to confirm that the commercial terms and product prices match approved expectations before routing the package.

  • Review from Innovate’s perspective as the buyer
  • Consider spend, criticality, data or system access, and supply-chain dependency
  • Route issues to accountable reviewers; keep approval with people
AGENT ROLE

Evidence-led decision support

The agent assembles a clause-level checklist, highlights departures from buyer guidelines, validates item prices when reference data is present, and prepares an auditable review package.

  • Quote or cite the contract section behind every finding
  • Call out missing exhibits, linked policies, and uncertain matches
  • Never approve, sign, or silently accept an exception

The investigation

From document intake to a human review package

The agent follows the agreement’s evidence, not assumptions. Review depth increases when spend, business criticality, data access, or operational dependency warrants it.

01 · IntakeInventory the package and its governing documents›
Identify the agreement type, parties, dates, term, renewal, order of precedence, supplied exhibits, and referenced-but-missing materials. Do not treat a linked policy as reviewed if it was not provided.Agreement · Order form · Exhibits
02 · TriageSet review depth from relationship risk›
For this sample, proposed price changes, short acceptance timing, and a missing security schedule call for enhanced review. System access, personal data, critical services, or custom technology would trigger specialist review.Vendor Contract Guidelines
03 · AssessCompare clauses and prices; record exceptions separately›
Assess applicable domains against the buyer’s standard, cite the clause, explain practical impact, and propose a correction or review question. Match products by item code first; flag unmatched entries and unexplained differences.Agreement · Guidelines · Price List
04 · RouteDeliver findings to authorized reviewers›
Route nonstandard legal terms to Legal and Procurement; unusual pricing to Finance and Procurement; and security matters to Security and the business owner. Keep exception rationale, compensating control, accountable owner, approver, and approval evidence on record.Human review required
Exception record: Sample §3.2 price-change right · risk: uncertain order cost · suggested control: fixed price or capped objective index with advance approval · reviewers: Procurement and Finance · approval evidence: not provided · status: pending review.

Content Aviator — illustrative contract review
Can the supplier change the price after we place an order?
In this illustrative agreement, §3.2 allows a price change with 10 days’ notice. That leaves the final order cost open to change. Consider fixing the accepted order price or defining an objective cap and requiring advance approval. Procurement and Finance should review; this is not an approval decision.

Did the proposed schedule match the approved reference prices?
The sample schedule’s NC-1001 price matches the reference. NC-1002 is 8% higher in the supplied demo data, and NC-1099 has no item-code match. I can’t determine whether either difference is authorized from these documents; route both to Procurement, with Finance reviewing the price impact.
